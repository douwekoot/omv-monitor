How advisories are raised

  • A finding means something is broken or unsafe. An advisory means it is worth a look but nothing is broken, or a value was not reported.
  • Rules run when a page loads, on the newest sample in Prometheus. The host script refreshes the posture values every 15 seconds and Prometheus scrapes every 15 seconds, so a change shows within about 30 seconds.
  • New findings are emailed (the host script checks every minute). Raised and cleared events are kept in the alert history. Advisories are only shown here.
  • Not raised on purpose: an idle WireGuard peer (the phone is often off the VPN) and the unused wlp3s0 interface.
